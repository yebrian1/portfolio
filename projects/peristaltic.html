RAL 2025

Bio-Inspired Pneumatic Modular Actuator for Peristaltic Transport

Authors: Brian Ye, Zhuonan Hao, Priya Shah, Mohammad K. Jawed

A modular donut-shaped soft actuator system that uses sequential radial and axial inflation to transport objects via peristaltic waves.

View Paper Watch Demo

Demo Video

Results & Performance

Control of peristaltic transportation. (a) The fundamental unit, composed of two compression modules at the top and bottom with a longitudinal module in the middle, enables object transport through sequence-based control. (b) Multi-level synchronization is achieved via pressure signal differences ∆P.

System Performance. (a) Multi-level synchronization in object transportation. (b) Performance comparison between module sequences: C–L–C reached 1.00 ± 0.11 mm/s and C–L–L–C reached 1.23 ± 0.06 mm/s.
